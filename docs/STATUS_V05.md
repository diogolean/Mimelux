# Mimelux V5

The logged-out home is three screens. Screen 1 is the existing login. Screen 2 is a split: a quieter “monetize attention” call with one action on the left, and a large slideshow of featured creators on the right. Screen 3 is three short notes on the creator model, the affiliate model, and the fact that this prototype does not charge. The extra stat, the editorial band, and the repeated featured blocks from V4 are gone.

The login and signup form keeps a left-to-right switch under the fields. Off, the default, enters as a visitor. On, it reveals Criador and Afiliado and routes to that onboarding or dashboard. Demo logins still infer the role from the email when the switch is off.

Creator profiles use a short cover, an overlapping avatar, and compact counts for photos, videos, locked items, and likes. The name, handle, truncated bio, and social actions sit above full-width subscription pills. Multi-month offers live in a collapsible Promoções block. Pedido especial sits under the plans, then Postagens and Mídias.
